How It Works

Buy and sell pre-loved fashion in four simple steps.

The Marketplace Flow

📦

List Your Item

Upload clear photos, write an accurate description, choose the item's condition and price, then publish your listing so buyers can easily find it.

🛒

Buyer Places an Order

Buyers browse the marketplace, view product details, and place an order for the item they want to purchase.

🚚

Pack & Ship

Once you receive an order, carefully pack the item and ship it using the order details provided by the platform.

✅

Order Completed

After a successful sale, the platform deducts a 10% commission and the seller receives the remaining amount.

FAQ

How do I create an account?

Click Sign Up, fill in your details, and start browsing or selling immediately.

How do I list a product?

Go to Sell, upload photos, set a price, and submit. Your listing goes live instantly.

How does shipping work?

Sellers ship items via TCS, Leopards, or M&P. Buyers receive a tracking number after checkout.

When do sellers get paid?

Sellers receive their payout after the order is completed, minus the 10% platform commission.
