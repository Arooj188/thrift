How It Works

From signup to delivery, here is exactly how Pakistan's thrift marketplace operates for buyers and sellers.

Buyer Journey

1

Create an Account

Sign up with your name, email, phone number, and address. You will receive a verification email—click the link to activate your account. Verified accounts can buy, sell, and track orders.

2

Browse & Discover

Explore pre-listed clothing and shoes from sellers across Pakistan. Filter by category, view item details, condition summaries, and photos. Ask sellers public questions before buying.

3

Add to Bag & Checkout

Add items to your shopping bag, review your order, and proceed to checkout. Enter your shipping details, choose a courier (TCS, Leopards, or M&P), and select Cash on Delivery or Bank Transfer.

4

Order Confirmation

Once your order is placed, you will receive an instant confirmation email with your tracking number, item details, and delivery address. The order status will update as it moves through the platform.

5

Shipping & Delivery

Sellers ship items via the selected courier service. You can track your order status in your account—from Paid to Shipped to Delivered. Keep an eye on your email for shipping updates.

6

Order Tracking

Visit the Track Orders page anytime to see your order history, current status, tracking codes, and delivery notes. This page is only accessible to authenticated users.

Seller Journey

1

List Your Item

After logging in, go to List Item for Sale. Upload photos, set a price, describe the condition, and provide your pickup address. Our AI assistant helps categorize your listing and improve the description.

2

Receive Orders

When a buyer purchases your item, you will receive an instant email notification with the buyer's details, shipping address, and tracking number. Prepare the item for shipment right away.

3

Ship & Earn

Hand the item over to the selected courier with the provided tracking number. Once the order is marked complete, 90% of the sale price goes directly to you. The remaining 10% is the platform commission.

Buyer & Seller Responsibilities

Buyer Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate shipping details at checkout
  • Be available at the provided address for delivery
  • Inspect items upon arrival and report issues promptly
  • Use the platform's tracking system for order updates

Seller Responsibilities

  • List items with honest descriptions and clear photos
  • Ship items within a reasonable timeframe
  • Provide accurate tracking information
  • Respond to buyer questions on product pages

Platform Commission

Thrift Marketplace charges a 10% commission on every successful sale. This covers payment processing, order management, buyer-seller matching, and customer support. Sellers receive 90% of the final sale price.
