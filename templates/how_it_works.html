How It Works

Buy and sell pre-loved fashion across Pakistan in a few simple steps.

Marketplace Flow

Step 1

Create an Account

Create a free account using your name, email, password, and WhatsApp number.

  • List items for sale
  • Contact sellers
  • Manage your own listings
Step 2

Browse or List Items

Buyers can browse all publicly available listings. Sellers can create listings by adding photos, item details, condition, price, and preferred buyer contact method.

Step 3

Contact the Seller

Only registered and logged-in users can contact sellers. This helps protect sellers' privacy and reduces spam. Buyers simply log in, choose a listing, and contact the seller using the seller's preferred contact method.

Step 4

Complete the Exchange

Once both parties agree, arrange payment directly, decide on delivery or meetup, and mark the transaction as complete. Thrift does not process payments or shipping.

Seller Responsibility

Once an item is sold, mark it as sold or remove the listing so buyers know it is no longer available.

Community & Safety

To keep the marketplace safe for everyone:

  • Buyers must create an account before contacting sellers.
  • Sellers should provide accurate descriptions and photos.
  • Thrift reserves the right to remove listings that are misleading, inappropriate, fraudulent, prohibited, or violate the platform's guidelines.

FAQ

Do I need an account to contact a seller?

Yes. You can browse listings without an account, but you must sign up or log in before contacting any seller.

How do I list an item?

Click "List Item for Sale", complete the form, upload photos, and publish.

How are payments handled?

Payments are arranged directly between buyers and sellers. Thrift does not process payments.

Does Thrift charge any fees?

No. Listing items and using the marketplace is completely free.

What happens after I sell my item?

Mark the listing as sold or delete it so other buyers know it is no longer available.
