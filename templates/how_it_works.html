How It Works

Buy and sell pre-loved fashion across Pakistan.

The Marketplace Flow

List Your Item

Upload clear photos, add an accurate description, choose the item's condition and price, then publish your listing for buyers across Pakistan to discover.

Connect with Buyers

Interested buyers can browse listings, ask questions, or contact you directly using your preferred contact method to discuss the item and arrange a purchase.

Complete the Exchange

Once both parties agree, decide on a convenient payment and delivery or meetup method. Thrift simply connects buyers and sellers—we don't process payments or handle shipping.

FAQ

How do I create an account?

Click Sign Up, complete the registration form, and you'll be ready to start buying or selling in just a few minutes.

How do I list an item?

Go to List Item for Sale, upload your photos, add the item details, set your price, and publish your listing instantly.

How does payment work?

Payments are arranged directly between buyers and sellers. We recommend using secure payment methods and confirming all details before completing a transaction.

Does Thrift charge any fees?

No. Thrift does not charge any platform fees. Sellers keep 100% of the agreed selling price.
